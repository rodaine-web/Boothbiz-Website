What is BoothBiz?

BoothBiz is an all-in-one CRM and business management platform being developed for photo booth operators. It connects inquiries, bookings, proposals, contracts, payments, client workspaces, marketing and operations.

Do I need a website to use BoothBiz?

No. Every paid plan is planned to include a branded Connect Page with links to your contact and booking forms.

Can I use BoothBiz with my existing website?

Yes. You'll be able to link to your BoothBiz forms and, where supported, embed them. Advanced CMS and SEO publishing will depend on your website platform and plan.

Does Starter include a full website?

No. Starter includes the Connect Page and forms, not a full website. Website templates are planned as a separate premium offering.

Where do contracts and the client workspace fit?

Contracts are part of Sales. The secure Client Workspace is part of CRM and connects agreements, invoices, payments, planning, approvals and galleries in one place.

Is BoothBiz available today?

BoothBiz is in development. Join the waitlist to hear about launch and early access. Prices and features shown are proposed.

Can I migrate from another CRM?

We plan to offer migration options, with supported formats and assistance confirmed closer to launch.
