A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things to know.

We’re building thoughtfully, with photo booth businesses at the center.

Is BoothBiz available now?

BoothBiz is in development. Early-access registration is not open yet. The product tour and questionnaire are previews; neither creates a business account.

How much will it cost?

Subscription plans and launch pricing are being developed. We’ll share confirmed details before you’re asked to pay. There is no payment or purchase in this preview.

Can I use more than one type of booth?

BoothBiz is being designed for businesses with different experiences and equipment. The optional questionnaire preview shows the equipment types we’re designing around.

Will it replace my photo booth software?

BoothBiz focuses on managing the business around your events. Your photo booth capture software is a separate tool. Planned integrations will be announced as they become available.

Can I bring my current CRM data?

The optional questionnaire preview includes questions about current tools and migration needs. Migration tools and support are being planned; no automatic import is promised at this stage.

What about payments and integrations?

Provider integrations are planned and currently deferred. The demo uses sample data and does not take payments or send campaigns.
